Case study: growing a tarot TikTok channel

A tarot expert had a small following and no clear direction. The goal was to reach a larger new audience (mostly women), build a recognisable personal brand and publish healing, educational content that viewers value.

35,362views on the best video
91.1%of traffic from the For You feed
9%+engagement rate

Strategy

  • Platform research and audience persona.
  • Content pillars mapped to the funnel (awareness, consideration, conversion), planned monthly in Google Sheets.
  • Detailed scripts, with AI used to sharpen titles and keywords for search.

Production

  • Raw footage cut in CapCut: gaps removed, sound and visual effects added, pacing tuned.
  • A matching thumbnail system designed in Canva.

Brief for recruiters

ClientA tarot reader with a small TikTok following and no clear content direction.
GoalReach new viewers (mostly young women), build a personal brand and turn viewers into booking clients.
My roleResearch, strategy, content plan, scripts, video editing and thumbnails. The research notes were written in Vietnamese, so the tables below summarise them in English.
OutcomeThe best video reached 35K+ views, mostly from the For You feed, with a strong engagement rate.

1. Which content works on this niche

Content typeViral potentialConversionCompetitionEase of production
HealingMedium-highMediumMediumEasy
MessageHighMediumHighEasy
Guidance (zodiac or card-based “reading” of a person)HighHighHighEasy
Entertainment and trendsHighLowLowMedium
Storytelling about cardsLowHighLowHard
Card meaningsMedium-highHighLowMedium
Knowledge sharingMediumHighLowHard

2. Audience and channel diagnosis

AudienceWomen (or LGBT) in Gen Z, 17–26, in Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City. Most active late at night.
What they feelOverthinking, fear of being hurt or choosing wrong, unsure about love, study or career.
What they wantReassurance that things will be okay, more than a fortune-telling prediction.
Channel problemsPosted too rarely, played it safe, repeated scenes and captions that can look like spam, and ran no ads.
RecommendationsPost 4–6 videos a week, add new content lines, run ads, and refresh the visual identity.

3. Monthly content plan by funnel stage

StageContent pillarGoal
AwarenessEntertainment and messages (short predictions, trends, humour)Attract new followers and raise reach
ConsiderationGuidance and knowledge sharing (zodiac readings, card meanings, tips)Build trust and expert credibility
ConversionGuidance and review content (client feedback, reading previews)Turn viewers into booking clients

Figures come from TikTok analytics, updated 26 May 2026.
